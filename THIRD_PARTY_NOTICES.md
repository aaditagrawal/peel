# Artwork

Starter stickers use Twemoji graphics, copyright Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, maintained by jdecked/twemoji. Graphics licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

- Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/tree/v17.0.2/assets/svg
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: transparent padding, resizing and WebP conversion.

The Peel app icon is original vector artwork in scripts/icon.ts.

WhatsApp integration implements the documented sticker interchange protocol. Peel is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
